📚 CHEAT SHEET: NOTA ARAHAN ASAS LINUX
1. Navigasi Direktori:
  • pwd, ls, ls -la, cd [folder], cd ..
2. Pengurusan Fail & Folder:
  • mkdir [nama], touch [fail], cp [asal] [baru], mv [asal] [baru], rm [fail]
3. Membaca & Menyunting Fail:
  • cat [fail], head [fail], nano [fail]
4. Masa & Sumber Sistem:
  • date, free -h, df -h, uptime
5. Rangkaian & Pentadbiran:
  • whoami, hostname, ip a, ping [ip], history, clear
